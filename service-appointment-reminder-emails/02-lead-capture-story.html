There are really only two reasons a service business stalls out:

1.  You can't get enough new leads in the door.

2.  You get leads, but they ghost you, ignore your follow-ups, or never close.

Different problem. Different fix.

We only work with two types of people — the ones who need a real capture system built from scratch (paid ads, funnels, lead magnets, the whole machine), and the ones who already get leads but are bleeding revenue at the conversion step (follow-up, nurture, sales process, automation).

If capture is your problem now, conversion will be your problem 90 days from now. Always.

Fix capture, and the flood of new leads will expose every weak spot in your follow-up overnight. The two are joined at the hip.

So the call isn't “which one do you need.” It's both, eventually. The real question is which one we attack first.

Have an honest answer ready when we talk: where are you actually stuck right now?
