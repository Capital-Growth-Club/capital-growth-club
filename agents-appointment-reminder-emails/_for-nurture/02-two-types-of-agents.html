Type 1 — Agents struggling to capture a steady flow of leads.

You close most of what you get. But the well runs dry. Some months are great, others are dead. You're at the mercy of referrals and whatever your brokerage hands you.

Type 2 — Agents struggling to convert the leads they have.

Your CRM is full. Your phone is ringing. But somewhere between “interested” and “signed contract” most of them ghost or go cold.

Different problem. Different fix. They look the same from the outside — flat revenue, frustration, the feeling that something is broken. But the move you make is completely different.

Most agents I talk to think they're a Type 1. After a few questions on the call, half of them turn out to be Type 2.

That's the first thing we'll figure out when we talk — which one is actually true for you.
